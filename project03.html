Project 3: Biomimicry

Biomimicry is the practice implementing and mimicking design's and processes found in nature and biology to solve problems. In this project for my Engineering 01 Class I had to choose an animal or something from nature and try to mimic one of its features. I choose the wings of a butterfly and tried to mimic its flapping motion. I faced a number of challenges while designing and building this project, primarly how to turn the one direction of rotation of the motor into a linear up and down motion of the wing that resets itself and the pivoting of it. In this I thought of traditional engines in how it pushes a piston up and down and the pivoting could occur on some axle. There were a number of other problems, such as trying to find the right lengths of each arm of the linkage to prevent it from getting caught on itself and setting up the enclosure to align the last arm. The wings also had to be connected together in the end to prevent them from falling too much.
